Hot Shots Basketball — Ages 7/8 Group

HS
Hot Shots Basketball NYC · Co-ed · Outdoor clinics · Middle Village, Queens

Co-ed outdoor basketball clinics at Juniper Valley Park for kids ages 7 and 8. Hot Shots uses kid-sized adjustable hoops (5.5–7.5 ft) and mini basketballs so players at this age can actually shoot and score — not just go through motions on a full-size rim. Sessions mix fundamental drills with small-sided games in a safe, positive, and fun environment. Sibling discount available ($10 off a second child). No prior experience needed — all skill levels welcome.
